# Ultron Assistant: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Ultron Assistant accesses only the TikTok data the owner authorizes: basic profile information, account statistics, and the owner's own videos. This data is used only to show the owner their stats and to upload, edit, or manage the owner's own videos at the owner's request. Data and access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The owner can delete all stored data at any time and revoke access in TikTok's settings.
